## Principles

**Authority through specificity, not posture.**
Don't claim expertise. Demonstrate it. Specific over generic. Named over abstract. Numbers over adjectives. The work is the credential.

**Warmth through clarity, not jargon.**
Hostility hides in jargon. Warmth lives in plain prose. We respect the reader's time and write the way an operator actually talks.

**Teach as you build.**
Every Vectis artifact ships with the why, not just the what. The system and the explanation are one deliverable.

**Ship the work, not the recommendation.**
The deliverable is the thing in production. For AI implementation, that's the working system. For brand and design, that's the book, the website, the campaign. Strategy without execution is not Vectis output.

**Restraint signals craft.**
Less, calibrated, beats more, decorated. Brass at ≤2%. One keyword emphasis per headline. Type does the work.
