# Vectis

A four-partner firm building AI implementation systems for SMBs and 1:1 AI fluency for individuals. Quietly global. Operator-led. *Engineered to compound. Designed to stand out.*

## Operating principle

Everything in this document is directional. Vectis is launching, not planning — which means we'll know more in three months than we know today, and the doc should evolve with what we learn. Anyone working from this file (including future Claude instances inside the Vectis project) is required to push back, suggest improvements, and flag when something written here is no longer the best call. Treat the lines below as the current best read, not as immutable law.

## Thesis

Most companies know they should be using AI but don't know where to start — or they vibe-code a stack of disconnected tools that don't talk to each other and don't compound.

Most senior professionals know AI is reshaping their craft but don't have a private way to get fluent without signaling weakness or sitting in a cohort with strangers.

Both buyers exist right now. Both have budget. Neither is well-served. Vectis is the firm that bundles AI implementation, brand systems, and operational design into one engagement for SMBs — and runs a discreet 1:1 advisory practice for the senior operators who want a private partner instead of a course.

The first-mover window is real. The category is overcrowded at the bottom (cheap AI automation agencies racing to the floor) and surprisingly thin at the top (fewer than ten firms globally hit 9/10 on positioning, design, and offer clarity combined). Almost no one is bundling AI + brand + ops at SMB scale. Almost no one is doing premium 1:1 AI coaching at a real retainer. Vectis can claim both lanes by shipping faster and at higher craft than the alternatives.

## Brand language — /vectis as system

The forward slash is a brand asset, not just a logo flourish. It signals command-line origin, technical precision, and modular extensibility. Locked as the official chrome:

- **Product voice:** `/vectis deploy`, `/vectis audit`, `/vectis align` — frame engagements as commands, not services
- **URL architecture:** `vectisbuild.co/audit`, `vectisbuild.co/residency`, `vectisbuild.co/journal` — every section sits under the slash naturally
- **The .co suffix is malleable.** Vectis Co. on letterhead. Vectis Command in product copy and CTAs. Vectis Collective for community moments and Residency cabinet language. Same root, different register per context.
- **Mono labeling pattern (locked):** `/VECTIS · T2 ALIGN`, `/VECTIS · CASE 003` — slash + dot separator becomes the proprietary chrome the way "FORWARD-DEPLOYED ENGINEER" is for Northslope. Use across site, deck, invoices, retainer reports.

This is genuine brand asymmetry — the slash works as logo, command, prefix, and frame all at once, and most competitors are stuck with bare wordmarks.

## Verticals

### 1. AI Implementation for SMBs (primary vertical)

The wedge service. Where most revenue lives in Year 1.

**ICP:** Founders and operators of $1M–$50M revenue companies. SMBs that have grown past their early systems and need to modernize how they operate, how they show up, and how they grow — without time, talent, or trust to hand the keys to a Big Four firm. English-first. US-anchored, IL/UK/EU welcome.

**The three-tier engagement architecture (mapped to operational tempo):**

- **T1 ALIGN — AI Diagnostic.** Paid scoped 2-week engagement. Audit of existing systems, identification of highest-leverage AI integration points, deliverable artifact the client owns. Acts as the discovery motion that converts to T2.
- **T2 ACCELERATE — Implementation Sprint.** 6–10 week build engagement. AI workflows, automations, integrations, brand/design where relevant. The flagship product.
- **T3 COMPOUND — Partnership Retainer.** Monthly recurring. Maintenance, optimization, new builds as the company evolves. The renewable revenue base.

**Delivery model — client-hosted, Vectis-built.** Vectis sets up the AI infrastructure inside the client's environment (their tools, their accounts, their data, their hosting). Vectis does not host client AI systems on its own infrastructure. This is the structurally correct call for a services firm — keeps Vectis off the hook for downtime, data security, and regulatory exposure it shouldn't carry. The proposal pattern is set in the Shua engagement and becomes the default. Engagement-letter IP language: client owns the deliverables (configs, workflows, custom GPTs, prompt libraries built for their business); Vectis retains methodology and reusable patterns.

**Pricing direction:** $5K/mo retainer floor for SMBs (above the YouTube-funnel maintenance tier; below fractional CAIO). Build fees variable — possibly priced as % of cost saved + value created (Sam likes; structurally complex; held in reserve for proven clients rather than public marketing). Exact tier dollars and structure are open and will be iterated as packages and site copy come together — see Open Questions.

**Bundled-not-broken-out:** brand systems and operational design are integrated into the implementation engagement when the client needs them, rather than sold as separate verticals. The bundle is the differentiation.

### 2. Vectis Residency (secondary vertical)

1:1 AI fluency for senior operators. The "secret AI superpower" / discreet advisory line.

**ICP:** Operators in the $150K–$350K income band who are 2–5 years from a key inflection — partner/VP track, AI displacement risk, or business expansion. Senior ICs, directors, fractional consultants, founder-operators of $1–10M businesses, mid-level professionals at law/finance/consulting firms. Psychographic filter: already paying $200+/mo for a coach, advisor, EA, or premium service. Not for early-career, students, or ML engineers.

**Why this works where cohorts don't:** cohorts can't calibrate to the buyer's actual P&L or book of business. Cohort enrollment also requires public LinkedIn signaling, which the senior operator buyer specifically wants to avoid. The Residency is private, persistent, and personalized. No one has to know.

**Format:** 1:1 monthly retainer, biweekly minimum cadence, async Slack/email between sessions. Tier dollars open — direction is roughly $650 / $1,200 / $2,500 monthly across Standard / Plus / Cabinet, with a one-time $1,500 Diagnostic on-ramp. Cabinet is invitation-only and capped.

**Standing artifact: the Vectis Operator Brief.** A single 1–2 page document built for each Residency client — their role, their goals, their tone preferences, their tools, their typical workflows. They paste it into ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini as the system prompt of their personal AI assistant. It's the deliverable that turns "you got coaching" into "you walked away with something you own." Modeled on Liz Baker's AI Coworker Blueprint pattern from the upskilling research. **Has not been built yet** — Sam and Tzvi build a v1 for each other (or a friend) as the working artifact before it goes on the site as a Residency deliverable. Without a real one, it's vaporware.

**Confidentiality posture (the buyer will ask before signing).** Three plain-English answers go in the engagement letter and on the `/residency` privacy section:
1. *We don't tell anyone you're a client.*
2. *We don't record sessions.*
3. *Testimonials use initials and role only — never your full name.*

This is the Reboot/Pivot Method pattern. Three sentences in a contract. Decide once, never improvise on a sales call.

**Site treatment:** secondary section on the homepage, dedicated `/residency` URL with quieter editorial register (bone surface inversion, GT Sectra display, generous whitespace). The discretion is reinforced by *how* it's presented, not just what it says.

## Brand & site direction

**Brand guide images in project knowledge are directional iteration sheets, not the locked visual system.** The active iteration set is Sam's prelim 10-page brand guide and Tzvi's one-page direction. Earlier explorations (Quiet Authority, Classical Marble) were cut — too Metis-adjacent (Sam's other brand) and too philosophical for the mentor/friend/partner register Vectis is targeting. The current direction described below is the working synthesis; the locked system lands during the V1 site build. When in doubt, this section overrides the images.

**Visual register:** sits between Linear (clean technical) and Anthropic (warm intellectual). Mercury, Resend, Cartesia, and U.S. Graphics Co. occupy this midpoint already. Vectis is joining a register, not inventing one.

**Type stack:** Söhne (body + UI), GT Sectra Display Italic (manifesto moments), Söhne Mono or Berkeley Mono (technical accents). Berkeley Mono is the brave swap — more proprietary fingerprint, in active use by Cartesia and Perplexity. Decision pending site-build mockups.

**Color:** Deep Ink #0E1116 primary surface (~70%), Bone #FAFAEA inverse / Residency primary (~25%), Mission White text only, Brass #A8896C accent ≤2% surface area at any time. Flat dark, no gradients. Zero box shadows.

**Site architecture:** one site, surface inversion to distinguish verticals. SMB section runs deep ink + sans display + T-state mono labels. Residency section runs bone + serif display + Roman numeral labels. Shared nav, type stack, brass accent, and footer.

**Motion:** three behaviors total. Hover state (150ms ease-out), section heading scroll-fade (250ms once), single T0 → T3 progression bar load animation (~600ms once). No marquees, no parallax, no scroll-jacking.

**Imagery:** maximum 3 photographs at V1. One Residency hero still-life (desk, notebook, brass object, warm light, studio-shot). One founder portrait when partners are public. Otherwise: ASCII diagrams, leader-line annotations, dimensional callouts, and typography. The construction-mark / spec-sheet aesthetic is the visual system.

**Anti-patterns:** no gradient blobs, no bento grids, no logo cloud, no Inter on dark gradient, no Cormorant Garamond, no documentary "operators at work" photography, no chatbot widget, no dark-mode toggle, no rounded corners over 4px.

Full reference scan, anti-references, and pre-launch build checklist live in Research Pass #3 in project knowledge.

## Risks

1. **Founder agreement collapses.** Highest-probability failure mode in any 4-partner firm. Mitigation: written agreement before Vectis takes a second client.
2. **Bundle thesis doesn't hold in delivery.** AI + brand + ops in one engagement is the differentiation, but it's also the operational risk — most agencies fail at this. Mitigation: Shua and the next 2 engagements are Sam-led with Tzvi on strategy; bundle offered selectively, not by default, until the delivery muscle is real.
3. **Tzvi bandwidth.** Working hours split across Vectis + Agentic Israel through year-end, with a public-facing constraint compounding it. Mitigation: Sam carries founder voice through year-end; Adam owns marketing execution; site build is Tzvi's owned workstream because it's the highest-leverage thing he can do invisibly.
4. **Capacity ceiling at client #2.** If Vectis is even mildly successful, two simultaneous SMB engagements stress the four-partner team immediately. Not a today-problem, but the talent bench (subcontractors, trusted IL devs, Adam's marketing freelancers) needs to be pre-built before it's needed.
