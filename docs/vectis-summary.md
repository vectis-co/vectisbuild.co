# Vectis

A four-partner firm building AI implementation systems for SMBs and 1:1 AI fluency for individuals. Quietly global. Operator-led. *Engineered to compound. Designed to stand out.*

## Operating principle

Everything here is directional. Vectis is launching, not planning — push back, suggest improvements, flag when something written here is no longer the best call. This file is the current best read, not immutable law.

## Thesis

Most companies know they should be using AI but don't know where to start — or they vibe-code disconnected tools that don't compound. Most senior professionals know AI is reshaping their craft but don't have a private way to get fluent without signaling weakness. Both buyers exist now, both have budget, neither is well-served. The category is overcrowded at the bottom and thin at the top — fewer than ten firms globally hit 9/10 on positioning, design, and offer clarity combined. First-mover window is real.

## Verticals

**1. AI Implementation for SMBs (primary).** Three tiers mapped to operational tempo: T1 Align (paid 2-week diagnostic), T2 Accelerate (6–10 week implementation sprint, the flagship), T3 Compound (monthly retainer). Brand systems and operational design bundled into the engagement, not sold as separate verticals. ICP: $1M–$50M revenue companies, English-first, US-anchored. $5K/mo retainer floor, exact tier dollars open. Delivery model is **client-hosted, Vectis-built** — Vectis sets up AI inside the client's environment; client owns deliverables, Vectis retains methodology.

**2. Vectis Residency (secondary).** 1:1 AI fluency for senior operators. ICP: $150K–$350K income operators 2–5 years from a key inflection (partner track, displacement risk, business expansion). Format: monthly retainer, biweekly minimum, async between sessions. Standing artifact — the **Vectis Operator Brief** — a 1–2 page document the client pastes into ChatGPT/Claude as their personal AI's system prompt. Has not been built yet; Sam + Tzvi build v1 for real before it goes on the site. Tier direction: $650 / $1,200 / $2,500 across Standard / Plus / Cabinet, $1,500 Diagnostic on-ramp. Cabinet capped, invitation-only. Confidentiality posture in the engagement letter: we don't tell anyone you're a client, we don't record sessions, testimonials use initials and role only. Open dollars.

## Risks

1. **Founder agreement collapses.** Highest-probability failure mode. Mitigation: written agreement before second client signs.
2. **Bundle thesis fails in delivery.** AI + brand + ops in one engagement is hard. Mitigation: bundle offered selectively until delivery muscle is real; first 3 engagements Sam-led.
3. **Tzvi bandwidth.** Working hours split with Agentic Israel through year-end + public-facing constraint. Mitigation: Sam carries founder voice; Adam owns marketing execution; site build is Tzvi's invisible high-leverage workstream.
4. **Capacity ceiling at client #2.** Two simultaneous SMB engagements stress the four-partner team immediately. Talent bench needs to be pre-built before it's needed.
